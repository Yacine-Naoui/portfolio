NestJS platform serving several insurance brands through a conversational channel for claims and quotes. A multilingual (FR/EN/AR) LangGraph agent handles claim declarations and quote requests with stateful memory, structured tool calls, vision-based damage assessment and fraud scoring. Plugin SDK, database-per-tenant isolation, RBAC, audit logging and a legacy Oracle ERP integration.
